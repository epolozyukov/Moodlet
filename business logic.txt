🎮 Tamagotchi-Style Calculation Rules (Classic Behavior)
1. Core Stats (0–100 scale)

Use these normalized values:

Stat	Meaning	Good direction
Hunger	fullness	lower is better
Energy	rest level	higher is better
Happiness	mood/play	higher is better
Hygiene	cleanliness	higher is better
Health	sickness level	higher is better

✅ Clamp all values between 0 and 100

⏱️ 2. Global Time Tick

Tick interval (classic feel):

Every 5 minutes real time → apply decay

Game must also process offline time

📉 3. Natural Decay Rules (every tick)

These numbers are tuned to feel like the original pacing.

Hunger

+4 every tick (pet gets hungrier)

If sleeping → +1 instead

Thresholds

60 → show "hungry" icon

85 → mood penalty

=100 → start health decay

Energy

−3 every tick while awake

+6 every tick while sleeping

Thresholds

<40 → sleepy warning

<20 → pet may auto-sleep

=0 → forced sleep + health risk

Happiness

−2 every tick

Additional −2 if hunger > 80

Additional −2 if hygiene < 30

Thresholds

<40 → sad face

<15 → refuses some actions

Hygiene

−3 every tick

−6 after eating (poop event chance)

Thresholds

<40 → dirty warning

<20 → health decay starts

Health (derived decay)

Health normally stable at 100.

Decrease health by −4 per tick if ANY:

hunger = 100

hygiene < 20

energy = 0

sickness untreated for >30 min

💤 4. Sleep Rules (IMPORTANT — classic feel)
Auto Sleep Trigger

Pet goes to sleep when:

energy ≤ 15
AND
not currently playing
Random Sleep Chance (classic quirk)

Every tick when:

energy < 30

→ 20% chance to fall asleep

While Sleeping

Effects per tick:

energy +6

hunger +1

happiness −0.5

cannot play or eat

can be woken by user

Wake Up Rules

Pet wakes when:

energy ≥ 90
OR

user manually wakes

After waking:

happiness −5 (grumpy penalty)

🍔 5. User Actions Effects
Feed

Meal

hunger −35

hygiene −8

happiness +4

30% chance poop event

Snack

hunger −15

happiness +8

health −3 (over-snack penalty)

⚠️ If hunger < 20 and user feeds:

happiness −3 (overfeeding dislike)

Play

Allowed only if:

energy > 20

not sleeping

not sick

Effects:

happiness +20

energy −10

hunger +6

Clean

hygiene → 100

happiness +3

Medicine (when sick)

health +25

sickness flag cleared

happiness −2

🦠 6. Sickness Rules

Pet becomes sick if ANY condition lasts ≥ 3 ticks:

hunger > 90

hygiene < 15

energy = 0

happiness < 10

When sick:

happiness −3 per tick

health −4 per tick

cannot play

🎂 7. Growth / Evolution Rules (classic style)
Stage timing
Stage	Time
Egg → Baby	1 hour
Baby → Child	24 hours
Child → Teen	48 hours
Teen → Adult	72 hours
Care Quality Score (important!)

Track rolling average:

care_score = average of:
  hunger < 60
  happiness > 50
  hygiene > 50
  sickness events
Evolution outcomes

Score ≥ 80 → best character

Score 50–79 → normal

Score < 50 → bad ending character

💀 8. Death Conditions (classic harshness)

Pet dies if:

health = 0
OR

left sick for > 12 hours
OR

hunger = 100 for > 6 ticks
OR

user neglect for > 72 hours

🔔 9. Attention Alert Logic

Pet calls for attention when ANY:

hunger > 70

energy < 30

hygiene < 40

happiness < 40

sick = true

But cooldown = 10 minutes between alerts.
